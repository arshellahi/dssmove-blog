Important — please read

  • Not financial advice. This calculator is provided for general information and educational purposes only. It does not constitute, and should not be relied upon as, financial, legal, or benefits advice. For tailored guidance, contact Citizens Advice, entitledto.co.uk, or a qualified welfare adviser.
  • Benefit estimates are indicative. Actual Universal Credit and other benefit awards depend on a DWP assessment of your full circumstances. Figures shown use 2025/26 published DWP rates, Local Housing Allowance tables, and the standard taper/cap formulas, but your real award may differ.
  • Council tax estimates — confirm with your local authority. The Band A rate shown is the published 2025/26 figure for your detected local authority. The actual property may be in a different band (B–H). The Council Tax Reduction (CTR) percentage is a national rough guide — every council runs its own scheme with different income tapers, capital limits, and exemptions. Check the actual band on gov.uk and contact the council to apply for CTR.
  • Utility estimates use UK averages. Energy figures are from Ofgem TDCV + Q2 2025 Price Cap; water from Ofwat regional averages; broadband assumes you qualify for the Ofcom social tariff (BT Home Essentials £15/mo). Your actual bills will vary by usage, supplier, and tariff. Contact your suppliers (or compare via Ofgem's Energy Compare) for a personal quote.
  • Food estimates use national essentials data (JRF Minimum Income Standard 2024 + ONS Family Spending). These cover a no-frills weekly shop only.
  • Property listings. Provided by Dssmove. We do not verify availability, condition, or landlord credentials — always view in person and conduct your own due diligence.
  • Nothing is stored. Everything in this calculator runs in your browser. Saved calculations stay in your browser's local storage on your device.
