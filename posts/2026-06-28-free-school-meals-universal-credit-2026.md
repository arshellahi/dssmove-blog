## What is changing in September 2026

Right now, you can get free school meals for your children if you claim Universal Credit **and** your household earns £7,400 or less a year after tax.

From September 2026, that earnings limit is being removed. Any family that claims Universal Credit will be entitled to free school meals in England, no matter how much you earn.

Over half a million more children are expected to become eligible once the change takes effect.

## How much you could save

Free school meals can save you up to £495 per child each year. That works out at roughly £41 a month per child — money that can go towards your rent, bills, and food at home.

If you have 2 children in year 3 or above, you could save close to £990 a year between them.

## How to apply

Free school meals do not start automatically. You need to apply through your child's school, even if you know you will be entitled.

Here is what to do:

1. **Contact your child's school office** and ask how to apply for free school meals.
2. **Give them your National Insurance number.** Most schools run an automated check that confirms your Universal Credit claim in seconds.
3. **Apply before September.** Schools can process applications over the summer holidays. Applying early means your child is registered to receive meals from the first day of the new term.

If your child already gets free school meals because your earnings are below £7,400, nothing changes for you — keep your current arrangement in place.

## Children in reception, year 1, and year 2

Children in the first three years of primary school already get free school meals regardless of your income, under the Universal Infant Free School Meals scheme. The September 2026 change applies mainly to children in year 3 and above.

## Check whether you qualify now

The new rules do not start until September 2026. If your household earns more than £7,400 a year, you do not yet qualify — but you will from September.

To check current eligibility or apply today, visit your child's school office or go to [gov.uk/apply-free-school-meals](https://www.gov.uk/apply-free-school-meals).
