If you are over State Pension age and renting privately on a low income, Pension Credit could make a real difference. More than 880,000 households across the UK are eligible but have never claimed it. The average award is worth over £3,900 a year.

## What Pension Credit does

Pension Credit is a weekly payment from the government that tops up your income to a guaranteed minimum. The main part is called Guarantee Credit.

Guarantee Credit sets a floor for your weekly income:

- **£238.00 a week** if you are single
- **£363.25 a week** if you are a couple

If your total weekly income — including your State Pension, any private pension, and other regular income — falls below those amounts, the government pays the difference. You do not need to be on any other benefit to qualify.

## How it helps with your rent

If you get Pension Credit and you rent privately, you can claim **Housing Benefit** from your local council to help cover your rent.

Since 1 July 2026, new Housing Benefit claims are only open to people who have reached State Pension age or who live in supported or temporary housing. If you are 66 or over, renting privately, and on a low income, Pension Credit is the gateway to that help.

You can claim Housing Benefit as part of your Pension Credit application. Tell the DWP Pension Service that you rent privately and they will pass your details to your local council.

## Why you should apply now

The Winter Fuel Payment qualifying week for 2026 to 2027 runs from **21 to 27 September 2026**. If you are in receipt of Pension Credit during that week, you will receive the Winter Fuel Payment automatically — between £100 and £300 to help with your heating bills this winter.

Pension Credit claims can be **backdated by up to 3 months**. If you apply in late July or August and your claim is approved, you could be in payment before that September qualifying week and also receive backdated payments.

## Who qualifies?

You may be eligible if you:

- live in England, Scotland or Wales
- have reached State Pension age (currently 66)
- have a weekly income below £238.00 (single) or £363.25 (couple)

Having savings does not automatically rule you out. Use the free Pension Credit calculator on GOV.UK to see where you stand.

## How to claim

Apply online at **gov.uk/pension-credit**. You can also apply by calling the Pension Credit claim line (free, Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm). When you apply, tell the adviser that you rent privately so that they can start your Housing Benefit claim at the same time.

Citizens Advice can check whether you are likely to qualify and help you complete the form for free.
