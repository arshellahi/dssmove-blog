The Warm Home Discount is a government scheme that can put £150 back in your pocket. If you are on Universal Credit and you pay your own energy bills, you could get this money off your electricity bill from autumn 2026.

## What the discount is

The £150 is taken straight off your electricity account. If your account is in credit, your supplier sends you a cheque or bank transfer instead. It is a one-off payment for this scheme year — not a loan and not taxable.

## Do you qualify?

You need to meet two conditions:

1. You are named on your home's electricity account — or your partner is.
2. You are on a qualifying benefit on the date the government sets for this scheme year.

Universal Credit is one of the qualifying benefits. Housing Benefit, income-based Jobseeker's Allowance, and Income Support are also on the list.

Your supplier also needs to take part in the scheme. Most large energy companies are required by law to offer it. If you are not sure, check your supplier's website.

## Do you need to apply?

For most people, the answer is no. The government matches your benefit records with your supplier's records automatically. If your records match, the £150 is applied to your account without you doing anything.

If the automatic check does not pick you up, your supplier will write to you to say you may be eligible and explain how to apply. Keep an eye on your post and email from your supplier from autumn 2026 onwards.

## What if you rent?

The key question is who pays the energy bills. If you pay them directly as the named account holder, you can qualify. If your landlord pays the energy bills and they are included in your rent, you are not the account holder and cannot claim through this route.

If that is your situation, speak to Citizens Advice. They can check whether there is other energy cost support you can apply for.

## What to do now

You do not need to do anything straight away. But it is worth checking a few things:

- You have an active account with a supplier that takes part in the scheme.
- Your details on your Universal Credit claim are up to date.
- Your supplier has your current address and contact details.

Visit gov.uk and search for "Warm Home Discount" to see the scheme rules and check that your supplier is taking part.
