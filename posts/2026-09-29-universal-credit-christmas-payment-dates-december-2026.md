Christmas 2026 falls on a Friday. This creates two bank holidays in December — Friday 25 December (Christmas Day) and Monday 28 December (the substitute for Boxing Day, because Boxing Day itself falls on a Saturday this year).

The DWP does not process payments on bank holidays or weekends. If your payment date lands on a non-working day, you will receive your money on the last working day before.

## When you will be paid

If your Universal Credit, Child Benefit, PIP, Carer's Allowance, or other DWP benefit is due on **any date from Friday 25 December to Monday 28 December 2026**, you will be paid on **Thursday 24 December 2026** — the last working day before four consecutive non-working days.

If your benefit is due on **Friday 1 January 2027**, you will be paid on **Thursday 31 December 2026** instead.

Any payment due on a different date is not affected — your money arrives on its normal day.

## The budget gap to watch

The early payment is not extra money. Your next payment still arrives on your usual date in January 2027.

This matters most if your regular payment date is 28 December. You will receive your December payment on 24 December — but your next payment does not arrive until your usual date in late January. That could mean your December 24 payment needs to last around 35 days. That is longer than a normal monthly cycle.

## How to plan ahead

When the payment lands on 24 December, set aside money for these things before spending anything else:

- rent due in late December or early January
- direct debits and standing orders coming out before your January payment
- food and essentials to cover the whole period until your next payment

If you use a prepaid card or have a savings pot, move money for January bills straight away so it does not get spent accidentally.

## If you are short before your next payment

Universal Credit claimants can ask for a **budgeting advance** through their UC journal. This is an interest-free loan of up to £812 if you have children, repaid from future UC payments over up to 2 years.

Citizens Advice can help you work out your options for free. Find your nearest branch at citizensadvice.org.uk.

Knowing these dates now gives you time to plan your Christmas budget before the festive rush begins.
