The government is moving everyone off older "legacy" benefits and onto Universal Credit (UC). Legacy benefits include Housing Benefit, income-based Jobseeker's Allowance (JSA), income-related Employment and Support Allowance (ESA), and Income Support.

If you still claim one of these, you may have received — or may soon receive — a letter from the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) called a migration notice. That letter tells you to switch to UC. This guide explains what to do.

## Your deadline

Your migration notice has a deadline date printed on it. That date is 3 months after DWP sent you the letter.

You must claim Universal Credit by that deadline. If you do, you qualify for **transitional protection**. Transitional protection is a top-up payment. It makes sure your total UC amount is not lower in cash terms than your old benefits were.

If you miss the deadline, you still have one extra month to qualify. DWP calls that extra window the "final deadline." After the final deadline, you can still claim UC — but you will not get transitional protection, and your payments may be lower.

## How to claim

You can claim online at [gov.uk/universal-credit](https://www.gov.uk/universal-credit). You will need:

- your National Insurance number
- your bank account details
- your current rent amount and your landlord's name
- proof of identity

Your first UC payment usually arrives around 5 weeks after you claim. If you need money before then, ask DWP for a new claim advance on your first day of claiming. You repay it through small reductions to your future UC payments.

## What happens to your Housing Benefit

Your Housing Benefit keeps being paid for 2 weeks after you claim UC. You do not need to pay that money back, and it does not reduce your UC.

After those 2 weeks, the housing element of your UC covers your rent instead. There is no gap in your housing payment.

## If you need more time

Contact the UC Migration Notice Helpline before your deadline passes. The helpline number is printed in your migration notice letter. DWP may give you extra time if you have a good reason — for example, if you are seriously ill or in hospital. You must ask before the deadline, not after.

## Get help now

Citizens Advice can work out what your UC amount is likely to be before you claim. They can also help you fill in the application if you find it difficult.

Do not delay. Missing your deadline can mean lower payments for several years.
