If you rent privately and were born on or before 27 June 1960, you could get between £100 and £300 this winter to help cover your heating bills. This is the Winter Fuel Payment, and it is not just for homeowners — renters qualify on exactly the same terms.

The qualifying week for winter 2026 to 2027 runs from **21 to 27 September 2026**. Your circumstances during that week decide whether you qualify and how much you receive. Now is a good time to understand what to expect.

## Who qualifies?

You are eligible if you were born on or before 27 June 1960 and you live in the UK during the qualifying week (21 to 27 September 2026).

You do not need to be on any particular benefit to qualify. Even if you are not on Pension Credit or Housing Benefit, you may still receive a payment automatically.

## How much will you get?

The amount depends on when you were born and who you live with:

- **£200** if you live alone, or no one else in your home qualifies, and you were born between 28 September 1946 and 27 June 1960
- **£300** if you or your partner were born before 28 September 1946
- **£100** if you were born between 28 September 1946 and 27 June 1960 but you live with someone born before 28 September 1946

If your total income is over £35,000, HMRC will take the payment back through the tax system. For most renters on Pension Credit or Housing Benefit, this does not apply — your income is almost certainly below that level.

## You are probably paid automatically

Most eligible people do not need to make a claim. If you already receive the State Pension, Pension Credit, or Housing Benefit, the government usually pays you automatically.

You should receive a letter in October or November 2026 telling you how much you will get. The money itself is usually paid in November or December.

## What if you need to claim?

If you think you qualify but you are not already receiving one of those benefits, you may need to make a claim. Claims for winter 2026 to 2027 open on **21 September 2026**. You can apply online at GOV.UK or by calling the Winter Fuel Payment helpline.

If you are unsure whether you need to claim or whether you are eligible, Citizens Advice can help. Their service is free and confidential.

## If the payment does not arrive

Do not assume you have missed out if your letter or payment has not arrived by December. Contact the Winter Fuel Payment Centre — the number is on GOV.UK — or go to your local Citizens Advice. They can check your record and help you make a late claim if something has gone wrong.
