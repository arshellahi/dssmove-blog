The Warm Home Discount scheme for winter 2026 to 2027 is now open. If you are on a qualifying benefit and your name is on an electricity account, you should receive a letter between October 2026 and early January 2027. It will tell you that £150 will come off your electricity bill before 31 March 2027.

## Who gets the payment automatically

The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) checks your benefit records and matches them to your electricity account. If your details match, your energy supplier contacts you. You do not need to apply.

Qualifying benefits include:

- Universal Credit (UC)
- Pension Credit
- Income-related Employment and Support Allowance (ESA)
- Housing Benefit

Not everyone on these benefits gets the discount. The DWP also looks at your income level and household circumstances to decide who qualifies.

## Two things to check right now

**1. Your name is on the electricity bill.** The automatic match only works if you or your partner are the named account holder. If your landlord pays the electricity directly, or if the bill is in a flatmate's name, the DWP cannot match your record. Ask your landlord or supplier how to get your name added.

**2. You have not moved recently.** If you moved home after 23 August 2026 — the qualifying date — your new address may not be in the DWP's records yet. Contact the DWP to update your address, and let your new energy supplier know.

## What to do if no letter arrives by mid-November

From mid-November 2026, you can call the free Warm Home Discount helpline:

**0800 030 9322** (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm)

Have your National Insurance number and electricity account number ready when you call.

You must call by the end of February 2027. After that, you cannot claim the £150 for this winter.

## If you are not on a qualifying benefit

Energy suppliers can also give the discount to households with a low income who are not on the main qualifying benefits. Availability is limited. Contact your electricity supplier directly to ask whether you can apply.

## Looking ahead

The government confirmed in 2026 that the Warm Home Discount will continue every winter until 2030 to 2031 at £150. If you miss the discount this year, the same scheme runs again next October. Getting your name on the electricity bill and staying on a qualifying benefit are the two things most likely to make you eligible automatically in future years.

If you are not sure whether you qualify, Citizens Advice can help you check at no cost.
