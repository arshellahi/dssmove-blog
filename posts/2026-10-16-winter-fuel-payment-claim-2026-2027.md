Winter is coming, and there is a government payment to help with your heating bills. If you were born on or before 27 June 1960, you could receive the Winter Fuel Payment for 2026 to 2027. Most people are paid automatically — but some will need to apply, and phone lines open from 19 October 2026.

## What is the Winter Fuel Payment?

The Winter Fuel Payment is a one-off, tax-free sum to help cover your energy costs over winter. You do not repay it, and you can spend it on anything you like.

## How much will you get?

The amount depends on your age and who lives with you.

If you live alone:

- **£200** if you were born between 28 September 1946 and 27 June 1960
- **£300** if you were born before 28 September 1946

If another eligible person shares your home, the amount for each of you will be lower. You should get a letter in October or November confirming exactly what you will receive.

## Do you need to claim?

Most people get the payment automatically. You do not need to apply if you already receive any of these:

- State Pension
- Personal Independence Payment (PIP)
- Attendance Allowance
- Carer's Allowance

You will need to make a claim if you have never received the Winter Fuel Payment before, or if you deferred your State Pension since your last payment.

**Phone claims open from 19 October 2026.** Call the Winter Fuel Payment Centre on **0800 731 0160**. The number is free. Lines are open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm. Have your National Insurance number and bank details to hand.

If you prefer to apply by post, download the claim form from GOV.UK and send it to: Winter Fuel Payment Centre, Mail Handling Site A, Wolverhampton WV98 1LR.

The deadline to claim for winter 2026 to 2027 is **31 March 2027**.

## What if your income is above £35,000?

If your total taxable income is above £35,000, HMRC may recover the payment through the tax system. But this does not apply if you receive Universal Credit, Pension Credit, or another means-tested DWP benefit — in that case, you keep the full amount.

Your partner's income is not counted towards the £35,000 limit.

## When will you be paid?

Payments are expected in November or December 2026. Once you know you are getting it automatically, you do not need to do anything else.

If you are unsure whether you need to claim, or you want free help checking your eligibility, visit citizensadvice.org.uk or call their helpline.
