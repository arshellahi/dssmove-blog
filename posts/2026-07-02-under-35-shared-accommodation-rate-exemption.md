If you are under 35 and single, Universal Credit (UC) usually only helps with the cost of a room in shared housing. This is called the shared accommodation rate (SAR). It does not matter if you rent a studio flat or a one-bedroom home — the DWP calculates your housing costs element using the SAR, not the higher one-bedroom rate.

The SAR is the lowest band of Local Housing Allowance (LHA). In many areas it is significantly less than the LHA rate for a self-contained home. If your rent is above the SAR, you must cover the gap yourself.

## You might qualify for a higher rate

The law sets out several groups who are exempt from the SAR. If you fall into one of the categories below, the DWP must use the one-bedroom LHA rate instead.

**You receive PIP, DLA or ADP.** If your Personal Independence Payment (PIP) includes the daily living component, you are exempt. The same applies if you receive the middle or high care rate of Disability Living Allowance (DLA), Adult Disability Payment (ADP) in Scotland, or Armed Forces Independence Payment (AFIP).

**You are a care leaver aged under 25.** If you were in local authority care for 13 weeks or more from the age of 14, you are entitled to the one-bedroom LHA rate until your 25th birthday.

**You have lived in a homeless hostel.** If you spent at least 3 months in a hostel for homeless people and accepted resettlement support during that time, you are exempt.

**You are an approved foster carer.** If you are a registered foster carer and either currently have a child placed with you, or have had a child placed within the last 12 months, you are exempt.

**You have experienced domestic abuse or modern slavery.** If you have been formally recognised as a victim of domestic abuse or human trafficking through the government's process, you are exempt. Contact Citizens Advice for guidance on the evidence the DWP needs.

## How to get your rate corrected

Tell the DWP through your UC online journal. Explain which exemption you believe you qualify for, and upload supporting evidence. For example: a PIP award letter, a letter from your local authority confirming care leaver status, or a letter from a hostel. The DWP should update your housing costs element once your exemption is confirmed.

If the DWP has already made a decision and you disagree, ask for a mandatory reconsideration. You have one month from the date of the decision.

## Get help

Contact Citizens Advice at citizensadvice.org.uk or Shelter at shelter.org.uk if you are unsure whether you qualify. Both provide free advice and can help you challenge a decision.
