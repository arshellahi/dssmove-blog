Cold weather is coming. If you claim Universal Credit and are not working, you could get £25 added to your benefit payment automatically every time the temperature drops. You do not need to apply.

The Cold Weather Payment scheme runs from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027. The money lands in the same bank account as your benefits within 14 working days of a cold spell ending.

## What triggers a payment

A cold snap counts when the average temperature at a weather station near your home is recorded as, or forecast to be, 0°C or below for 7 days in a row. Each separate cold spell earns you £25.

If there are 3 cold spells this winter, you could receive up to £75. Payments are automatic for each qualifying period — you do not need to request them.

## Who qualifies on Universal Credit

You will usually qualify if you claim Universal Credit and none of the adults on your claim are working. One of these must also apply:

- You or your partner have a health condition or disability and have limited capability for work
- You have a child under 5 living with you

You will also qualify if your Universal Credit claim includes a disabled child amount. For this, it does not matter whether you or your partner are working.

## Other benefits that qualify

Cold Weather Payments also go to people claiming:

- Pension Credit
- Income-related Employment and Support Allowance (ESA)
- Income-based Jobseeker's Allowance (JSA)
- Income Support

If you are on one of these benefits, you are covered automatically in the same way.

## How to make sure you get paid

Payments are automatic, but a few simple steps now can make sure nothing goes wrong before November:

- Check your address is correct on your UC claim. The payment is linked to a weather station near your postcode — an out-of-date address could mean a missed payment.
- Make sure your bank details are up to date. The money arrives in the same account as your other benefits.
- If you have a child under 5 or a health condition, check these are recorded on your claim.

## If a payment does not arrive

It can take up to 14 working days after a cold spell for the money to reach you. If you believe you qualify but have not been paid, contact Citizens Advice. They can check your eligibility and help you chase a missing payment with DWP.
