Good news if you have children and you claim Universal Credit (UC). From September 2026, every child in a UC household in England can get free school lunches. No income test. No earnings cap.

This is a big change. Right now, your child can only get free school meals if your household earns less than £7,400 a year after tax — as well as claiming UC. That limit has not moved since 2018. Many families on UC have missed out because their earnings sit just above that line.

## What changes in September 2026

From the start of the 2026–2027 school year, the income cap goes. If you claim UC, your child is entitled to free school lunches — whatever your household earns.

More than 500,000 extra children across England are set to qualify under the new rules.

Children in Reception, Year 1, and Year 2 already receive free school lunches through a separate scheme. The September 2026 change mainly helps families with children in Year 3 and above.

## Two types of free school meals

From September, there are 2 categories:

- **Targeted free school meals** — for children in UC households with income of £7,400 a year or less. Schools also receive Pupil Premium funding for these children, which pays for extra support.
- **Expanded free school meals** — for children in UC households earning above £7,400. The meals are still completely free. Schools do not receive Pupil Premium for this group.

Both children get the same lunch every day. The difference is about school funding, not what is on your child's plate.

## How to apply

You can register your child before September. Do not wait — applying early means your child is set up from the first day back.

To apply, contact your local council or check your council's website. You will need your National Insurance number. Most councils use an automatic check to match your number against your UC record, so you usually will not need to send in paperwork.

You can also ask your child's school how to apply in your area. Schools are preparing for the change now.

## Not sure if you qualify?

If you are unsure, get free advice from [Citizens Advice](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk). They can check your situation and help you apply.

If you already claim UC and have children in Year 3 or above, it is worth registering now so everything is in place for September.
