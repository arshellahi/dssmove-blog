The Holiday Activities and Food (HAF) programme gives children from low-income families free activities and a hot meal every day during school holidays. Summer sessions are still running — here is how to get a free place for your child.

## Who can get a place?

Your child can attend free HAF sessions if they are:

- aged 4 to 16
- enrolled for **benefits-related** free school meals at their school

Benefits-related free school meals are for children whose families claim Universal Credit or certain other qualifying benefits. Ask your child's school if you are not sure — they can check quickly.

**Important:** all children in Reception, Year 1, and Year 2 get a free school meal through a separate scheme. This does not count for HAF. Your child also needs to be enrolled for benefits-related free school meals to qualify for a holiday place.

## What does each session include?

At each session your child gets:

- a free hot meal every day they attend
- a range of activities — sport, arts, cooking, music, and outdoor games
- a safe, supervised environment during the holiday

Children can attend for up to 16 days over the summer. Sessions are run by local councils through community groups, charities, and schools.

## How to book

1. Contact your local council to find out what sessions are still running near you.
2. Ask your child's school for a voucher code to book online.
3. Book as soon as you can — places in late August fill fast.

If you are not sure whether your child is enrolled for benefits-related free school meals, your child's school is the quickest place to check. They can confirm eligibility and hand over a voucher code at the same time.

## What if sessions near you are fully booked?

Ask your council if any dates are still available — councils often release extra spaces in the final weeks of the summer holidays.

If you need help with food costs more generally, ask your council about the Crisis and Resilience Fund. Citizens Advice can also point you to local food banks and other support close to you.

## What changes in September 2026

From September 2026, all families claiming Universal Credit in England will qualify for free school meals regardless of earnings. This means more children will be eligible for HAF places from the 2026 to 2027 school year onwards.

If your child does not qualify this summer, they may qualify from September. Ask their school to confirm when the new rules take effect.
