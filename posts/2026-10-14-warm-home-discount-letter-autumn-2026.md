The Warm Home Discount scheme has reopened for winter 2026 to 2027. If you are on Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, Pension Credit or income-related Employment and Support Allowance (ESA), a £150 electricity discount could be heading your way — automatically.

From this month, energy suppliers are starting to send out letters to eligible households. The payment goes straight to your supplier to reduce your electricity bill, or gets added to your prepayment meter. You do not need to do anything. The discount must reach you by 31 March 2027.

## Who qualifies

Your eligibility was decided on 23 August 2026, the qualifying date for this year's scheme. Two things had to be true on that date:

- you or your partner was receiving Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, income-related ESA, or Pension Credit
- your name or your partner's name was on the electricity bill for your home

If both apply, your energy supplier should write to you between October and mid-December to confirm your discount.

You do not need to earn less than a certain amount to qualify. Eligibility is based on receiving one of the qualifying benefits on the qualifying date — not on your income level or savings.

## What to do if your letter has not arrived

Letters go out at different times depending on your energy supplier. If you have not received anything by mid-December:

1. **Check your supplier is part of the scheme.** Not all energy suppliers take part. You can find the list on the GOV.UK Warm Home Discount page.
2. **Call the Warm Home Discount helpline.** It reopened in October 2026 and the number is on the GOV.UK page.
3. **Contact Citizens Advice.** They can check your eligibility and explain your next steps, for free.

The most common reason people miss out is a mismatch between the electricity account and the benefit claim. If the name on the electricity bill is different from the person receiving the qualifying benefit, the payment may not go through. Contact your supplier to check and update your account if needed.

## If you pay by prepayment meter

If you pay for electricity using a prepayment meter, the £150 will be added as credit to your meter automatically. No action needed — but look out for a letter telling you when the credit will appear.

## What the discount does not cover

The Warm Home Discount is for electricity only. It does not reduce your gas, water, or other bills. If you are worried about heating costs and do not qualify for this discount, Citizens Advice can check what other winter energy schemes and emergency support might be available in your area.
