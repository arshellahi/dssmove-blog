If you are over 66 and renting your home, one benefit could make the biggest difference to your housing costs: Pension Credit.

Pension Credit tops up your weekly income. It brings it up to at least £238 a week if you live alone, or £363.25 a week if you have a partner. It can also unlock Housing Benefit to help pay your private rent — yet hundreds of thousands of people who qualify are not claiming it.

On 13 October 2026, the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) launched a Week of Action to find those missing out. DWP is sending letters to areas where many eligible pensioners have not yet put in a claim.

## Do you qualify?

You may qualify if:

- you are over State Pension age (currently 66)
- your weekly income is below £238 if you live alone, or £363.25 if you have a partner

Your income includes your State Pension and any private pensions. Savings below £10,000 do not count as income at all.

If your income is just above those amounts, you may still qualify. DWP can add extra amounts if you are a carer, have a disability, or have certain housing costs. The upper limit where you stop qualifying can be higher than the headline figure.

## Why it matters if you rent

When you get the Guarantee Credit part of Pension Credit, you can also claim Housing Benefit to help pay your private rent.

Normally, Housing Benefit is not available if you have more than £16,000 in savings. But with Guarantee Credit, that savings limit is removed. So even if you have been turned away from Housing Benefit before, it is worth checking again now.

Your landlord does not need to know you are claiming. Housing Benefit can be paid directly to you or, if you prefer, straight to your landlord.

## What else Pension Credit unlocks

Claiming Pension Credit can also get you:

- £150 off your electricity bill through the Warm Home Discount
- £25 Cold Weather Payments for each week temperatures stay below freezing in winter
- A reduction on your council tax bill

You do not need to apply for most of these separately. DWP checks your eligibility for linked benefits automatically once you claim Pension Credit.

## How to apply

Go to GOV.UK and search for "Pension Credit". You can apply online or call the free Pension Credit claim line — the number is on the GOV.UK page.

If you are not sure you qualify, Citizens Advice can check for free and help you put in a claim. Shelter can also advise you about Housing Benefit if you need help with your rent.

Do not wait too long to apply. Pension Credit is not paid automatically — you have to put in a claim. If you have been putting it off, this week's DWP campaign is a good prompt to check.
