When someone close to you dies, the last thing you want to worry about is money. But funerals are expensive, and if you are on a tight budget, finding the funds can feel overwhelming.

There is a government payment called a Funeral Expenses Payment that can help. It is run by the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) and is separate from your regular Universal Credit (UC) or Housing Benefit payments.

## Who can get it?

You may be able to claim if you or your partner are receiving any of these benefits at the time of the funeral:

- Universal Credit
- Housing Benefit
- Income Support
- income-based Jobseeker's Allowance
- income-related Employment and Support Allowance
- Pension Credit
- Working Tax Credit (the disability or severe disability element)
- Child Tax Credit

You also need to be the person responsible for arranging the funeral. This usually means you are the partner, a close relative, a close friend, or someone who cared for the person who died.

## What does it pay for?

The payment can cover:

- burial fees or cremation fees in full
- the cost of moving the body within the UK if it needs to travel more than 50 miles
- reasonable travel costs for you to arrange or attend the funeral
- death certificates and other official documents
- up to £1,000 towards other funeral costs, such as the coffin and funeral director's fees

The payment will not usually cover the full cost of a funeral. Any money from the person's estate — such as savings or property they left behind — will be taken into account and may reduce what you receive.

## How to claim

The quickest way is by phone. Call the DWP Bereavement Service on **0800 151 2012** (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm). You can also apply online or by post using the SF200 form — find it by searching "funeral payment claim form" on GOV.UK.

You can claim **before** the funeral if you already have a signed invoice from the funeral director. This can make it easier to manage the cost in advance.

## Do not miss the 6-month deadline

You must submit your claim within **6 months of the date of the funeral**. After that, DWP will almost always refuse the claim.

If you are still waiting for a decision on a qualifying benefit, do not wait. Put in your Funeral Expenses Payment claim now — DWP will hold it until your benefit decision comes through.

## Need more help?

If you are unsure whether you qualify, or you would like help filling in the form, contact Citizens Advice. They offer free, impartial advice at citizensadvice.org.uk or over the phone at their local helplines.
