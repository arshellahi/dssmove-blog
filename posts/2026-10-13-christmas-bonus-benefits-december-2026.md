## What is the Christmas Bonus?

The Christmas Bonus is a one-off £10 tax-free payment from the government. It is paid to people who receive certain disability and carer benefits.

You do not need to claim it. If you qualify, the bonus is paid automatically into your usual bank account in December. It normally arrives during the first full week of December. On your bank statement, look out for 'DWP XB' as the reference.

## Which benefits qualify?

You must be receiving at least one qualifying benefit during the first full week of December. The benefits most relevant to renters on Universal Credit are:

- **Personal Independence Payment (PIP)** — either the daily living or mobility component
- **Disability Living Allowance (DLA)** — if you have not yet moved to PIP
- **Carer's Allowance** — if you spend at least 35 hours a week caring for someone who gets PIP or DLA
- **Attendance Allowance** — for those over State Pension age with care needs
- **State Pension or Pension Credit** (Guarantee Credit)

Universal Credit on its own does not qualify for the Christmas Bonus. But if you get UC and also receive one of the benefits listed above, you will receive the bonus automatically.

You must also be in the UK, Channel Islands, Isle of Man or Gibraltar during the qualifying week.

## Does it affect your other benefits?

No. The £10 Christmas Bonus does not count as income. It will not reduce your Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, Council Tax Reduction, or any other support you receive. The full £10 is yours to keep.

## What if the bonus does not arrive?

If you think you qualify but the payment has not appeared in your bank account by 1 January 2027, take action quickly:

- If your benefits are managed through Jobcentre Plus, call them and report the missing payment.
- If you receive the State Pension or Pension Credit, contact the Pension Service instead.

You have until 1 January to report a missing bonus for that year. After that date, you may not be able to claim it.

## Where to get more help

Not sure whether you qualify? [Citizens Advice](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk) offers free advice and can review your full benefits picture. If you do not yet claim PIP but live with a long-term health condition or disability, you may be entitled to it — and that would bring you within scope for the Christmas Bonus too. Use the free benefits calculator at [Turn2us](https://www.turn2us.org.uk) to check what you might be entitled to.
