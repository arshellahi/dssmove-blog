If your landlord takes you to court, you do not have to face it alone. Free legal help is available at every stage — even if you have never used a solicitor before, and even if your income is too high for ordinary legal aid.

## The Housing Loss Prevention Advice Service

The Housing Loss Prevention Advice Service (HLPAS) is a government-funded service that gives you free legal advice if you are at risk of losing your home. You can use it from the moment your landlord first threatens court action — you do not need to wait for a court date.

It does not matter how much you earn. The service is free to everyone.

An adviser can help you:

- understand the notice your landlord has sent you
- check whether the notice was served correctly
- put together a defence or a repayment plan if you are in rent arrears

## Free help on the day of your hearing

If your case reaches court, a duty adviser will usually be at the courthouse on the day of your hearing. You do not need an appointment. Arrive at least 30 minutes early so you have time to speak to them before you go before the judge.

The duty adviser can speak to the judge on your behalf, help you agree a repayment plan, and make sure you understand what is happening at every step. This help is completely free.

## More support from October 2026

A new fund launched on 1 October 2026 means that 48 advice organisations across England and Wales — including Citizens Advice — can now offer more free in-person and online help to people facing eviction, benefit disputes, and problem debt.

If you have received a court summons or are falling behind on rent, you do not need to wait for a hearing date before you ask for help.

## What to bring to court

If you do have a hearing, bring:

- your tenancy agreement
- the notice your landlord gave you
- court letters
- proof of your income or benefits

Having these documents ready means an adviser can help you more quickly on the day.

## Where to get help now

- **Citizens Advice** can help you check your notice, understand your rights, and prepare for court. Find your nearest office at citizensadvice.org.uk.
- **Shelter** has a free housing helpline and advisers who can guide you through the whole process at shelter.org.uk.
