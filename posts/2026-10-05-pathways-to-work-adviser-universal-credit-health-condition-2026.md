## Free support is already available now

If your Universal Credit includes a health element because your Work Capability Assessment found you have a limited capability for work, your Jobcentre has a specialist called a Pathways to Work adviser ready to help you.

These advisers are based in every Jobcentre in England, Scotland and Wales. They offer free, voluntary, one-to-one support. There is no pressure to take up work or do anything you are not ready to do.

A Pathways to Work adviser can help you:

- Think through your options at your own pace
- Find free skills training, such as IT courses or sector-specific programmes
- Learn about work-based opportunities that fit around your condition
- Build confidence before you decide whether to try any paid work

You do not have to wait for your work coach to raise it. You can ask at your next Jobcentre appointment or send a message through your UC online journal.

## More is coming for under-25s in April 2027

On 27 September 2026, the government announced that the Jobs Guarantee will expand to young people under 25 who are on Universal Credit with a health condition.

From April 2027, if you are under 25 and your Work Capability Assessment has been completed, your Jobcentre will offer you a guaranteed job. The offer is voluntary — you choose whether to take it.

If you do take the job, the government will pay your employer 100% of your wages for up to 25 hours a week for 6 months. You will also get mentoring and support with your mental health, finances and housing during that time.

The job offer will be made at week 13 of your claim after your Work Capability Assessment.

## Trying work will not put your benefits at risk

The Right to Try rules that came into force on 30 April 2026 mean that working alone cannot trigger a reassessment of your UC health element or your PIP. If a job does not work out, your support stays in place.

You also have a Work Allowance. If your UC includes a housing costs element, you can earn up to £427 a month before your UC payment reduces at all. After that, UC reduces gradually at 55p for every £1 you earn — it does not switch off in one go.

Knowing this may make it easier to talk to a Pathways to Work adviser now, before April 2027 arrives.
