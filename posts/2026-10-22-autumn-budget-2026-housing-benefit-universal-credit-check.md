The Autumn Budget is on Wednesday 28 October 2026. Chancellor John Healey will set out the Government's tax and spending plans for the year ahead.

If you get Universal Credit (UC) or Housing Benefit and rent privately, the Budget is one of the main events that can change how much housing support you receive.

## What private renters should watch for

Two figures affect how much you get toward your rent.

**Local Housing Allowance (LHA)** is the cap on how much housing support you can get as a private renter. LHA rates have been frozen at April 2024 levels. That means they have not kept up with rent rises in most parts of England. Groups including the Local Government Association have called on the Government to reset LHA to the 30th percentile of local rents — making the support go further.

**The benefit cap** limits the total benefits a household can receive each month. Outside London, it is set at £1,835 a month for families with children. It has not changed for several years. An increase would give capped families more room to pay their rent.

## What to do before 28 October

There are two practical steps you can take this week.

**Find your LHA rate.** Search GOV.UK for the Valuation Office Agency rate finder. Enter your postcode and the number of bedrooms you need. The figure shown is the most your UC housing costs or Housing Benefit can cover for a private rental in your area.

**Do a free benefits check.** Turn2us and Citizens Advice both offer free online calculators. A check takes around 10 minutes and shows whether you are claiming everything you are entitled to. Doing this now gives you a clear picture before any Budget changes take effect.

## After the Budget

Budget announcements do not change your payments straight away. Most benefit changes take effect from April of the following year.

After 28 October, search GOV.UK for "Budget 2026" to find a summary of what was announced. If you are unsure what any change means for your claim, contact Citizens Advice. They offer free, confidential advice on benefits.
